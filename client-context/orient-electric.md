## Pain Points & Requirements

### Current Challenges
- **Cost**: 60-70% of contact center budget goes to tier-1 support calls
- **Attrition**: High turnover for both support and sales agents
- **Training**: Diverse product portfolio (fans vs lighting) requires different expertise; high training overhead with frequent turnover
- **Language**: Coverage limitations with current manual processes

### Primary Use Case: Inbound Complaint Registration
- Simple 5-step process to log tickets in CRM
- Want to start with this basic use case, then expand
- Focus on simple queries: warranty, basic support

### Secondary Use Case: Sales Agent Support
- Outbound telephony automation across diverse product portfolio
- Additional sales revenue potential from automation

---

## Technical Evaluation Criteria
- **Low latency** critical for Indian market patience levels
- Want **purpose-built voice solution** vs "plug and play" approaches stitching multiple APIs
- **Multi-context switching** capability (e.g., support issue → product inquiry mid-call)
- **Integration flexibility** with existing AWS systems
- **On-premises deployment** option for future cost optimization
- **VPC deployment** for faster go-live
