## Technical Requirements (Aviation)

### Airline-Specific Needs
- **High Reliability:** Flight operations cannot go down
- **Real-Time:** Booking and status updates must be immediate
- **PNR Handling:** Passenger Name Record number accuracy critical
- **Flight Codes:** Accurate airport codes, flight numbers
- **Multilingual:** International routes require multiple languages
- **24/7 Operations:** Airlines operate round-the-clock

### Languages
- **English** - Primary (international)
- **Hindi** - Secondary (domestic passengers)
- **Regional Indian languages** - Domestic routes
  - Tamil (Chennai hub)
  - Malayalam (Kerala routes)
  - Kannada (Bangalore hub)
  - Others based on route network

### Integration

- Reservation system

- Contact center platform
- CRM for customer data
