## Key Insights from Earlier Calls (Jan 23)

### Two buyer personas (useful for qualification)
- **Type A: Business buyer**
  - Wants cheapest solution, free POC, procurement drags
  - Will compare "rupees per minute", tends to be POC-fatigued
- **Type B: Technical buyer (CTO/CPTO/CDO)**
  - Burnt by vendors, building internally, wants self-serve capabilities
  - Faster time-to-value; stronger conversion tendency

**Implication for Smallest:** prioritize **Type B** conversions when possible; for Type A, be strict on paid pilots and avoid endless free POCs.

### Free POC stance (aligned with our ICP discipline)
- Industrial segment asks for free pilots frequently.
- Arun's default: **no free POC** unless funded by hyperscaler/partner; will support demos/canned proofs, but avoid 3-month free runs.

### EY operating reality (important for partner motions)
- EY is **not homogeneous**; multiple partners may reach out independently.
- Best practice: funnel investments through **one channel owner** to avoid "death by a thousand cuts".
- "White-label" / speed-to-value matters because implementation effort is high while "voice" is getting commoditized and compared on unit economics.
