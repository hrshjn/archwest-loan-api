## Collections Use Case Focus

### Why Collections is Entry Point
- **Clear ROI:** Reduce Days Past Due (DPD), improve recovery rates
- **High Volume:** Collections calls are ongoing need
- **Measurable:** Success metrics easy to track (collection %, DPD reduction)
- **Budget:** Collections teams have budget (impacts bottom line)

### Success Metrics to Propose
1. **Collection Rate:** % increase in successful collections
2. **DPD Reduction:** Reduce average days past due
3. **Cost per Call:** Reduction vs. human agents
4. **Right Party Contact (RPC):** Improved contact rates
5. **Promise to Pay (PTP):** Increased commitment rate
