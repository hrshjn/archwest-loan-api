## The Proof: WER (Word Error Rate)

### What is WER?
- **Word Error Rate** = % of words transcribed incorrectly
- Lower is better (10% WER = 90% accuracy)
- Industry standard metric for STT quality

### Smallest vs. Competition (Indian Languages)

| Provider | Hindi WER | Tamil WER | English (Indian Accent) WER | Cost |
|:---------|:----------|:----------|:----------------------------|:-----|
| **Smallest** | ~8-12% | ~10-14% | ~6-10% | $0.01-0.02/min |
| Google STT | ~15-20% | ~18-25% | ~8-12% | $0.024/min |
| AWS Transcribe | ~18-22% | ~20-28% | ~10-15% | $0.024/min |
| Deepgram | ~12-15% | ~16-20% | ~8-12% | $0.025/min |
| 11 Labs | N/A (TTS only) | N/A | N/A | $0.30/min (TTS) |

**Key talking point:**
> *"We're not just cheaper—we're more accurate on Indian languages because that's what we trained for. Let's run your actual call recordings through both systems and compare."*
